# References for manuscript.md, with verification sources

> This work was produced as an exercise in testing whether AI agents can run a bioinformatics research project. A lead AI agent (the 'PI', Claude Opus 5.5) directed sub-agents (Claude Opus 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5) under a human-written brief. The human researcher supplied the brief, the data and PhosphoSitePlus access; they did not write the code, run the analyses, or write the text. All results should be checked before use.

Checked on 2026-10-27. Numbering matches the manuscript (PLOS/Vancouver style, order of first citation; first six authors, then "et al."). "Verified" means that authors, title, journal, year, volume, pages or article number and DOI in the manuscript entry were checked against a primary or index record (mostly the Crossref record of the DOI; otherwise bioRxiv, DataCite, figshare, PyPI or the publisher's proceedings page). Fields that no record confirmed are left out or marked "[verify]". The project files named in the earlier version of this table (`docs/lit/*.md`, `LITERATURE.md`, `DATA.md`) were the starting point; where they disagreed with the publisher record, the record was used (see the last section).

Refs 9, 16, 18, 21, 32, 34, 35, 41, 46, 48, 50, 51, 52, 53, 54 were added at the PI's request for tools and resources that the manuscript names without a citation; refs 11, 12, 19, 20, 23, 30, 33 replace URL, resource or incomplete placeholders with the formal publication. Most added works are named in project files as tools, but their bibliographic records come from the external sources listed here, not from the project files.

## Inconsistencies found while checking

- iKiP-DB first author: the publisher record (doi:10.1021/acs.jproteome.2c00198) lists Mari T, Mösbauer K, Wyler E, Landthaler M, Drosten C, Selbach M. `docs/lit/02_ks_annotation_sources.md` l.119 and `docs/lit/03_phosphoproteomics_sources.md` l.120 ("Mari et al. 2022") are right; `docs/lit/01_methods_benchmarks.md` l.250 ("Kuznetsova/Bantscheff et al.") is wrong.
- KolossuS (ref 8): the DOI resolves to "Deep learning-coupled proximity proteomics to deconvolve kinase signaling in vivo"; KolossuS is the model introduced in that preprint, not its title.
- Jiang et al. 2025 (ref 36): article number is 2766 (was "[verify]").
- Ochoa et al. (ref 14) and Reinecke et al. (ref 40) were published online a year before their print issue; the print year is used. The bioRxiv KolossuS preprint (ref 8) has no peer-reviewed version recorded in Crossref as of this check.
- SIGNOR (ref 16): the external test took SIGNOR rows through OmniPath; the SIGNOR release in that snapshot is not recorded, so the citation of SIGNOR 4.0 rather than 3.0 (doi:10.1093/nar/gkac883) should be confirmed by the publishing researcher.
- DepMap (ref 33): the figshare item is "Chronos parameters (Public 26Q1)", version 2; DepMap's own preferred dataset citation could not be checked because the depmap.org portal is blocked here (`DATA.md`).
- Platt (ref 50): the book is dated 1999 in some indexes and 2000 in others; year left "[verify]".
- Supplement S1 §1.6: the Müller-Dott citation now gives the verified title, volume and article number and points to main-text ref 2.
